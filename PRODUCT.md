# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A patient or client who has been handed a technical medical/lab report (blood panel, diagnostic result, etc.) and cannot easily interpret it themselves.

## Product Purpose

Reports Analyzer takes a patient's own medical report and returns a plain-language explanation, term by term, so they understand it before acting on it or discussing it with a clinician.

## Positioning

Explains the patient's own real report, in the language of their actual document, rather than a generic health-info article about the topic.

## Operating Context

Standalone marketing/demo page, to be hosted on a subdomain of beyondchats.com. No live backend in this build — the primary flow is a fully worked sample, not a functional upload.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Scope is strictly patient/medical report analysis. No insurance, financial, or legal document analysis — that positioning has been explicitly removed. An AI model drafts each explanation; a clinician reviews the templates used for report types. No live upload/backend exists yet in this build.

## Brand Commitments

Must read as visually consistent with the parent company site beyondchats.com (https://beyondchats.com/): deep navy/charcoal base, teal/cyan primary accent, warm orange secondary accent, sans-serif type throughout, rounded/pill buttons, iconography and product-mockup visuals over photography, a numbered-step device for process explanation.

## Evidence on Hand

A generic blood-panel (CBC/thyroid) sample report is the demo content — synthetic, not real patient data, clearly a worked example. No real testimonials, logos, or case studies on hand — do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

Show the real product before describing it. Second person, plain language. Brevity over completeness — say it once. One clear next step per section. Never overstate certainty about a medical result.
